*A Court of Thorns and Roses*, by Sarah J. Maas, follows Feyre Archeron, a 19-year-old huntress who kills a faerie wolf to feed her starving family. As retribution, she is taken across the wall into the magical realm of Prythian by Tamlin, a beast-like High Lord of the Spring Court. There, she discovers a dangerous and beautiful world, and her feelings for Tamlin evolve into a fiery passion as she realizes a wicked shadow is growing and threatening to destroy his world.

Major Moments:
* **The Hunt**: Feyre kills a large wolf in the woods, not realizing it is a faerie in disguise.
* **The Beast Arrives**: Tamlin arrives at Feyre's cottage demanding retribution and takes her to the Spring Court.
* **Life in the Spring Court**: Feyre learns about the faerie world, meets Lucien, and discovers the curse afflicting the Spring Court where everyone must wear masks.
* **Growing Close**: Feyre and Tamlin begin to fall in love, despite the danger and her initial hostility.
* **The Blight**: Feyre becomes aware of the ancient, wicked shadow spreading across Prythian that she must find a way to stop.
