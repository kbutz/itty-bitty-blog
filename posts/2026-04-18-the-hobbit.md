*The Hobbit*, by J.R.R. Tolkien, follows the journey of Bilbo Baggins, a comfortable and unadventurous hobbit whose life is turned upside down when the wizard Gandalf and a company of thirteen dwarves arrive at his door. They enlist Bilbo as their "burglar" to help them reclaim their ancestral homeland, the Lonely Mountain, and its vast treasure from the fearsome dragon Smaug.

Major Moments:
* **The Unexpected Party**: Gandalf and the dwarves arrive at Bilbo's home, drawing him into their quest.
* **Trolls, Goblins, and Gollum**: The company encounters trolls and is later captured by goblins. Bilbo gets separated, finds a mysterious magic ring, and plays a high-stakes game of riddles with the creature Gollum.
* **Mirkwood and the Spiders**: The group travels through the dark and dangerous forest of Mirkwood, battling giant spiders and getting captured by Wood-elves.
* **The Lonely Mountain and Smaug**: Bilbo sneaks into the mountain and converses with the dragon Smaug. Smaug attacks the nearby Lake-town but is slain by Bard the Bowman.
* **The Battle of Five Armies**: A massive conflict erupts over the dragon's treasure, involving men, elves, dwarves, goblins, and wargs.
* **Return Home**: Bilbo returns to the Shire, forever changed, and finds his belongings being auctioned off, as he was presumed dead.
