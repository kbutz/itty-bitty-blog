*The Blacktongue Thief* by Christopher Buehlman is a dazzling, fast-paced fantasy adventure filled with crazy magic and innovative styles of witchery.

Plot Summary:
The plot revolves around Kinch Na Shannack, a low-ranking member of the Takers Guild who owes them a small fortune for his education as a thief. This crushing debt drives him to lie in wait by an old forest road, planning to rob the next traveler that crosses his path. Unluckily for Kinch, that poor sod turns out to be Galva, a handmaiden of the Goddess of Death and a survivor of the Goblin wars.

Character Summary:
* **Kinch Na Shannack**: A 13th-century thief and our narrator, heavily in debt to the Takers Guild.
* **Galva**: A handmaiden of the Goddess of Death and a survivor of the Goblin wars that decimated the lands.

Major Moments:
* **The Debt**: We learn Kinch owes a small fortune to the Takers Guild for his education, driving him to desperate measures.
* **The Ambush**: Kinch waits by an old forest road to rob the next traveler, which sets the plot in motion.
* **Meeting Galva**: The traveler Kinch attempts to rob turns out to be Galva, a handmaiden of the Goddess of Death.
