**Key:** D
**Source:** Clyde Davenport

Kicked off the Fall stringband session with "Shooting Creek." Clyde Davenport’s bowing always has such a great groove to it. It's a fantastic D tune to sink into, especially finding those rhythmic pockets on the cello underneath the fiddles.
