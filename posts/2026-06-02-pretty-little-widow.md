**Key:** A
**Source:** John Ashby

"Pretty Little Widow" (or "Pretty Little Widder") is a driving traditional fiddle tune from the repertoire of Virginia fiddler John Ashby of Free State (an area near Warrenton, VA). The tune has deeper roots in old-time string band music and was first cut by Gid Tanner and His Skillet Lickers in 1928 as a comic rave-up. It was also performed by Stringbean on The Porter Wagoner Show in the 1960s with an electric guitar and clawhammer banjo sound.

Here are a few notable recordings to check out:

* [John Ashby - Pretty Little Widow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4QzSd_lMsP0) (Original Virginia Fiddler recording)
* [Galax Bogtrotters - Pretty Little Widder](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1LnfQOYVhiA) (A fine cover learned from John Ashby's playing)
* [The Skillet Lickers - Pretty Little Widow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wrZ0iBV4LDA) (The 1928 recording)
