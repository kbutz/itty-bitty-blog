In the second installment of the *Dungeon Crawler Carl* series, Carl and Princess Donut face the Over City on the third floor of the dungeon. The training levels are over, and the game truly begins. Carl becomes a Primal and chooses the Compensated Anarchist class, while Donut selects the Former Child Actor class. They must navigate a sprawling metropolis devastated by a mysterious calamity, dealing with an undead circus, a new quest system, and increasingly dangerous mobs while trying to keep their viewers entertained.

Major Moments:
* **Class Selection**: Carl becomes a Primal with the Compensated Anarchist class, and Donut stays a cat with the Former Child Actor class.
* **The Over City**: Carl and Donut enter the third floor, navigating the devastated metropolis and trying to find a medium settlement.
* **The First Quest**: They receive their first quest: "Show Must Go On", and fight lemurs and a clown before entering a safe room.
* **New Loot**: Donut receives a Clockwork Triplicate and a cat tree, while Carl gets the Cesta Punta skill, Louis L'Amour books, and a cactus.
* **Grimaldi's Traveling Circus**: Carl and Donut scout out the undead circus as night falls.
* **Tsarina Signet**: Escaping magical mortar fire, they meet Tsarina Signet, an Elite.
