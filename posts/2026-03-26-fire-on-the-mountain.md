**Key:** A

**Source:** Isham Monday

Fire On The Mountain from fiddler Isham Monday (1879–1964). Monday was a fiddler from Monroe County, Kentucky, widely regarded as one of the most archaic-sounding fiddlers ever recorded. He learned to play at age 10, and his style shows considerable Scottish influence, characterized by vigorous bowing, heavy bow pressure, and the fiddle tuned a whole tone or more below concert pitch. He played for local dances and parties from his teens through roughly World War II, when he set the fiddle aside until folklorists connected with him. He was recorded by D.K. Wilgus in Monroe County in 1959, preserving a repertoire of tunes that remains influential in old time music circles today. This tune is unique to Isham Monday and is one of his better-known tunes. It is not related to other “Fire on the Mountain” tunes, which Monday also played, under a different name.
