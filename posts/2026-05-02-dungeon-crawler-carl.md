*Dungeon Crawler Carl*, by Matt Dinniman, is an absolutely wild and addictive journey. When every human-made structure on Earth mysteriously collapses, sinking into the ground, our protagonist, Carl, finds himself thrust into a sadistic, 18-level intergalactic game show dungeon. To survive, Carl—accompanied only by his ex-girlfriend's prize-winning show cat, Princess Donut—must navigate deadly traps, battle bizarre monsters, and most importantly, keep the countless viewers across the galaxy entertained. The apocalypse has never been so chaotic or televised.

Major Moments:
* **The Collapse**: Earth's surface structures vanish, and Carl, wearing only his boxers and a leather jacket, is forced into the dungeon with Princess Donut.
* **The Safe Room**: Carl and Donut figure out the basic mechanics of the dungeon, inventory, and classes. Donut unexpectedly gains sentience and a voice.
* **Goblin Bulldozer**: Carl begins his unorthodox approach to combat, often using the environment and explosives rather than traditional weapons.
* **The Meth-Dealing Llamas**: An encounter with a rival syndicate of drug-dealing llamas that highlights the sheer absurdity of the dungeon's inhabitants.
* **The Guild Hall**: Carl and Donut make their first major alliances and enemies, establishing themselves as prominent "crawlers" in the game.
* **The First Boss Fight**: A massive, explosive confrontation that tests all the skills and items Carl has accumulated, solidifying his reputation among the viewers.
