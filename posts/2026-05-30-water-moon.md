Water Moon by Samantha Sotto Yambao is a captivating magical realism novel set in a hidden pawnshop in Tokyo. Rather than trading material goods, this pawnshop trades people's regrets and life choices, which manifest as luminescent birds. When 21-year-old Hana Ishikawa inherits the pawnshop from her father, Toshio, she quickly realizes something is terribly wrong: the shop has been ransacked, a precious choice stolen, and her father is missing. Joined by a charming physicist named Keishin, Hana embarks on a whimsical and dangerous journey through a magical world governed by the monstrous Shiikuin to find her father and uncover the dark secrets behind her family's trade.

## Story Arc & Timeline of Major Moments
- **The Inheritance and the Intrusion:** Hana wakes up to inherit the pawnshop only to find it ransacked, a glowing bird (a choice) missing, and her father gone.
- **Meeting Keishin:** Keishin, a physicist from the normal world, enters the shop and offers to help Hana find her father.
- **Entering the Magical World:** They jump into a magical pond and arrive at a temple, learning that Toshio is searching for Hana's mother, Chiyo, who was thought to have been executed by the Shiikuin but was actually sentenced to eternal longing.
- **Fleeing the Shiikuin:** As they search for Hana's parents, they flee the fearsome Shiikuin. Hana struggles with her predetermined destiny and her betrothal to Haruto, who eventually encourages her to follow her feelings for Keishin.
- **The Subterranean Field & Dark Truths:** Hana and Keishin find Toshio and Chiyo in a subterranean field. They learn that the "choices" the pawnshop collects are pieces of clients' souls, which are given to soulless children to make them normal. Children who don't get enough choices become the monstrous Shiikuin.
- **A New Path:** Keishin suggests stopping the collection of choices to end the cycle. When the Shiikuin attack, Hana and Keishin escape, leaving her parents behind by their own choice. Hana and Keishin part ways after returning to the pawnshop.
- **Epilogue:** Five years later, Keishin and Hana reunite at an airport. Hana reveals she has closed the pawnshop, ended the cycle of the Shiikuin, and crossed over to Keishin's world permanently.
