**Summary:**
*Klara and the Sun* explores themes of humanity, identity, and technology in a dystopian future. The novel is told from the perspective of Klara, a solar-powered Artificial Friend (AF) who is purchased by a sick girl named Josie to be her companion. Throughout the story, Klara tries to understand the human condition and goes on a quest to ask the Sun to heal Josie.

**About the Author:**
Sir Kazuo Ishiguro was born in Nagasaki, Japan, in 1954 and moved to Great Britain when he was five. He is a celebrated British novelist and screenwriter known for his moving, deeply emotional works. In 2017, he was awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature.
